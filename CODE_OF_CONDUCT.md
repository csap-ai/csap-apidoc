# Code of Conduct / 行为准则

## Our pledge / 我们的承诺

We as members, contributors and maintainers pledge to make participation in the CSAP Apidoc community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

作为成员、贡献者和维护者,我们承诺:无论年龄、体型、是否有残障、民族、性别特征、性别认同与表达、经验水平、教育程度、社会经济地位、国籍、外貌、种族、宗教或性取向,每个人参与 CSAP Apidoc 社区时都不会受到骚扰。

## Our standards / 行为标准

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment / 有助于营造良好环境的行为:

- Being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences / 尊重不同的观点和经验
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback / 给出并虚心接受建设性的反馈
- Focusing on what is best for the community / 关注对社区最有利的事情
- Showing empathy toward other community members / 对其他成员保持同理心

Unacceptable behavior / 不可接受的行为:

- Sexualized language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention / 带有性意味的言语或图像,以及不受欢迎的性关注
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks / 挑衅、侮辱或贬损性言论,人身攻击或政治攻击
- Public or private harassment / 公开或私下的骚扰
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission / 未经明确许可公开他人的隐私信息
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting / 其他在专业场合中可被合理认为不当的行为

## Enforcement / 执行

Instances of abusive, harassing or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported to the maintainers at **support@csap.ai**. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly, and the privacy of the reporter will be respected. Maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions that do not align with this Code of Conduct, and may temporarily or permanently ban contributors for behavior they deem inappropriate.

如遇辱骂、骚扰或其他不可接受的行为,请发邮件至 **support@csap.ai** 向维护者报告。所有报告都会被及时、公正地审查和处理,并对报告人的隐私保密。维护者有权删除、编辑或拒绝不符合本准则的评论、提交、代码、Issue 及其他贡献,并可视情况暂时或永久禁止相关贡献者参与。

## Scope / 适用范围

This Code of Conduct applies within all community spaces (the repository, issues, pull requests, discussions) and when an individual is officially representing the community in public spaces.

本准则适用于所有社区场合(仓库、Issue、Pull Request、讨论区),以及代表本社区出现在公开场合时。
